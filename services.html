Limo & Black Car Service in Miami

Most people searching for a limo service in Miami don't want a stretch limousine — they want a private car service that shows up early and stays as long as the day runs. The Process

Three steps between you
and a handled day.

Step One

Tell us the movement

Date, pickup, party size, and anything that matters — a call, a message, or the quote form. Two minutes is plenty.

Step Two

Dispatch builds the plan

Vehicle, chauffeur and — where needed — protection are assigned. Flights are tracked, routes are run in advance, timing is confirmed back to you.

Step Three

Follow it live, then ride

You receive your tracking link before pickup. Watch the vehicle approach, message the chauffeur through it, and step out when it arrives.
