About Blueprintle

One desk in Fort Lauderdale.
A team wherever you land.

Blueprintle Service LLC is a chauffeur, private protection and ground-logistics company. Three services that most firms sell separately run here as one operation, coordinated from a single dispatch desk.

Our daily ground is South Florida — Miami, Fort Lauderdale and the Palm Beaches — and our work is mostly long-hour charter: a vehicle and a team that stay with the client for the day, the evening, or the whole stay. Every vehicle in service is model year 2025 or newer, and partner vehicles dispatched under Blueprintle mean a booking is never limited by fleet size.

The operation does not end at the county line. Blueprintle teams have run multi-day, multi-city movements across the United States — principals, entourages and security details moving through several cities on one plan and one point of contact. And through all of it, one rule holds: absolute confidentiality. No client names, no photos, no exceptions.
