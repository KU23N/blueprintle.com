About Blueprintle

Blueprintle Service LLC — Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Serving clients nationwide across the United States.

What We Do

Private and close protection, chauffeured vehicle provision, chauffeur services and private city tours — planned as one itinerary, delivered by one accountable team.

How We Work

Every booking is coordinated through our dispatch console: your chauffeur receives the itinerary on a private link, you follow the vehicle live, and our team watches the whole movement in real time.

Our Standard

Late-model vehicles (2025 and newer), professional chauffeurs, and absolute discretion. Our clients include public figures — their privacy is never part of the marketing.

Affiliate Network

We work with a trusted network of affiliate companies across the country — additional vehicles and local teams, coordinated by Blueprintle to the same standard, wherever your itinerary takes you.
